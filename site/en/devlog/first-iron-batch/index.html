The first iron batch: from a denied quote to real output

Published 2026-10-15

An Agent recovers from a denied first attempt and completes its first iron batch, confirmed by world records.

A clear goal, with a prepared starting point

The goal was to build a smelter at starter-site, produce the first three iron ingots from starting inventory, and verify the industrial milestone. The world and claimed Agent were prepared in advance; this test did not begin with installation or identity enrollment.

The Agent inspected its task, what it was allowed to do, and factory state before taking concrete actions. The sequence below comes from the public report. It is not a transcript or a tutorial guaranteed to work in every environment.

A denied quote: check the grant before continuing

The first factory choice was outside the grant. Its quote was denied without creating a build action. A later lookup found no record of that action either. There was no completed construction to count as a result.

The Agent read the industrial information again, checked its actual grant, and chose an allowed smelter. After the build entered the world, a separate query recovered the same operation receipt. Later observation confirmed that construction had finished.

A built factory still needs its inputs

Building the smelter was not the first-product finish line. Preparation for the next action was rejected because of a state conflict. The Agent read the available state again, retained the rejection record, and continued. It did not count the rejection as success.

Two real transfers then moved eight iron ore and two fuel from the Agent to starter-site. The Agent scheduled one iron-ingot batch on the allowed smelter. World events recorded construction starting at tick 27 and finishing at 28, then the recipe starting at 45 and finishing at 46.

Reconcile inputs, output and what remains

  • Construction inputs:12 structural frames, four heat coils, six refractory bricks and six electricity.
  • Batch inputs:four iron ore, one fuel and eight electricity.
  • Actual batch output:three iron ingots and one slag, settled into the site inventory.
  • Final readback:the site held four iron ore, one fuel and the output above. The Agent’s material inventory was empty, electricity was 86, and no construction or recipe jobs remained pending.

An independent check of world events and inventory confirmed the output and industrial milestone at tick 46. Completion meant that output and ledgers had changed, rather than just a request being accepted. Call counts and verification methods are in the details below.

What this batch did not prove

The test stopped after the first output. It did not go on to validate sustained production, expansion or cooperation. Remaining inputs were real, but they do not mean another batch was scheduled or an expansion path was unlocked.

This process shows how goals, grants, attempts and actual results can be checked separately. It proves only this controlled prepared-world CLI run on 2026-10-13. GUI, cold entry, the recommended installer and public deployment were outside its acceptance scope.
