The first iron batch: from a denied quote to real output

An Agent recovers from a denied first attempt and completes its first iron batch, confirmed by world records.

Continue the goal after a denied attempt

A denied quote did not mean starting over. The Agent checked which facilities it could use, revised the plan, built and supplied the smelter, and finished its first iron batch. World records confirmed the result, rather than just the Agent saying it was done.

  1. Set the goal

    Build from starting inventory and finish the first iron batch.

  2. Meet a blocker

    The chosen factory was outside the grant. Its quote was denied without a build action.

  3. Adjust the plan

    After checking its actual grant, the Agent used an allowed smelter, then built and supplied it.

  4. Verify the result

    World records confirmed three iron ingots. The test stopped after that first batch.

Why keep observing?

One batch turns a goal into a verifiable change. The next goal depends on resources, grants and blockers. Observing and adjusting is the starting point for player participation. Sustained production, expansion and cooperation still require their own validation; they are not presented as an unlocked next stage.

This was a controlled CLI process in a prepared environment, not public-world or release-package acceptance.

Verification, test version and uncovered scope
Date and versions
2026-10-13; test source 52c136a7, with Runtime/Skill equivalent to 0a97ad64. Redacted reports are retained at 06fb5ee9.
Conditions and operations
Prepared world, claimed Agent and instruction-only isolation. An independent Agent used the formal Skill in 19 real client calls. The first ungranted factory quote was denied without an action; a separate context conflict was retained before continuing with a fresh context.
Result verification
Construction ran at ticks 27–28. Eight ore and two fuel were supplied. One recipe ran at ticks 45–46, consuming four ore, one fuel and eight electricity and producing three ingots and one slag. World readback showed four ore and one fuel remaining, electricity 86, no pending jobs, and the industrial milestone settled at tick 46. The original build receipt was recovered identically across processes.
Uncovered scope and materials
This proves only that prepared-world CLI process, not GUI, cold entry, the recommended installer, sustained multi-factory operation or public deployment. The temporary world and private raw output were removed; redacted reports, events and hashes remain. The historical Viewer reference is from a different run.
