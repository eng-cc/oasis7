# Historical local PM materials

Existing memories, notes, task records and local caches remain readable here. They do not authorize development or establish delivery completion. Do not erase or regenerate user materials as part of routine work or cleanup.

Ordinary development uses Git, PRs, actual CI results and review records under the [development workflow](../doc/engineering/workflow/source-of-truth.md). Issues and Projects remain optional tracking tools. The former PM executors and templates are retired; historical commands can be inspected in Git history.
